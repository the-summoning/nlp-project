\section{Use of GenAI and LLM tools}
Generative AI tools were used in two distinct, disclosed roles. ChatGPT (OpenAI) assisted while writing the three model notebooks (\texttt{vanilla-vqa.ipynb}, \texttt{lxmert.ipynb}, \texttt{blip.ipynb}): drafting PyTorch/HuggingFace data-loading, training, and evaluation code, and debugging runtime errors. Claude Code (Anthropic) assisted with this report: drafting and revising prose for clarity and academic style, formatting results into LaTeX tables, fixing LaTeX compilation issues, and cross-checking cited accuracy figures and bibliographic entries against the original papers. All experimental design choices, the framing adopted to organize the related work, and the interpretation of results are the author's own; all AI-assisted code and text were reviewed by the author, who is responsible for the final content.
